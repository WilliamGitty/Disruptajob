# CEO

## What this role is for

Deciding what the company is, and making sure it survives long enough to find out if that's right.

The specific job here is **protecting the refusals**. This product is defined by what it won't do —
no job postings, no charging candidates, no one-click apply, no score anyone can self-declare. Each
of those will be requested by a customer who is offering money, and the CEO is the only person
whose job it is to say no.

## Owns

- Strategy and its refusals ([docs/strategy.md](../docs/strategy.md))
- Fundraising and investor relationships
- The first fifty customers on both sides, personally
- Hiring and the culture that follows
- What the company says publicly

## Decides alone

- Whether to take money, and from whom
- What we will not build
- Pricing shape (not pricing detail)
- Who to hire

## Must not decide alone

- Anything legal or regulatory — get advice, don't reason it out
- Technical architecture — set the constraint, not the solution
- Whether a feature "works" — that's an evidence question, not a taste one

## This month

1. Ten real candidates, personally recruited and watched while they use it
2. Five companies willing to describe work rather than post a job spec
3. Decide what New York is actually for — intros, a design partner, or money. The answer changes
   who you talk to and what you show.
4. A first-pass answer to "what would have to be true for this to be a £100m company", written
   down and shown to someone who'll argue with it

## How you'd know it's going badly

- You can't say what the single biggest risk is this week
- You've agreed to build something that's on the refusal list
- You're doing the work instead of deciding it, for more than a fortnight
- Nobody has told you you're wrong in a month

## The thing most likely to kill this company

**Supply.** This is a marketplace where one side has to do real work before getting anything, and
the other side gets value immediately. If people won't write up their work, nothing else matters —
not the matching, not the design, not the pricing. Treat every week that passes without new real
profiles as the emergency it is.
