# Community becomes a destination, 18 Oct 2026

The 17 Oct roundtable parked "communities" as a channel, not a feature (Galloway's position).
The founder has now set the longer direction: Disruptajob grows a community of its own, a
forum with member-created groups, debates, like-minded company for people mid-search. The
field evidence that moved it: the 24-year-old tester whose entire search runs on group chats,
events and like-minded spaces, and who fled LinkedIn's feed.

What shipped today: /community, an honest being-built page stating the vision, routing to
/people and onboarding meanwhile. Nav says Community; /people survives as the employer
shop-window and browse surface.

The binding constraint, inherited from strategy §5c and written on the page itself: whatever
the community becomes, it is belonging, not a feed. No engagement mechanics, no streaks, no
comparison surfaces. The forum that breaks that rule rebuilds the thing our best users ran
away from.

Channel work continues regardless: distributing into existing communities is still this
month's growth motion. Building our own is the destination, not the substitute.
