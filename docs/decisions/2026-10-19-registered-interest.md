# Registered interest: a hand-raise that is consent, not an application, 19 Oct 2026

The founder asked for a register-your-interest button on every role, plus employer sections
built around it. The danger named before building: a free one-click interest pile recreates
Easy Apply, the flood our own first article condemns. What shipped instead keeps the spine:

**The design.** Registering interest on a role does exactly one thing: it lets THAT company
see this candidate's profile, hidden or not, for as long as the interest stands. No form, no
message, no document, nothing lands in an inbox. Companies still find people through
evidence-ranked search; interest appears as a marker on people who already match, and as a
count on the role. Withdrawal is one click and deletion is the mechanism.

**The two asymmetries, mutation-tested in discovery.test.ts:** the grant matches company
names STRICTLY (fuzzy matching may deny access, never give it), and the blocklist beats a
standing interest, because blocking is the later, harder promise. Interest in a closed role
grants nothing. The privacy page was updated in the same commit, as the rule requires.

**Deferred with the employer rebuild:** the founder's sketched dashboard (interested /
contacted / interested-and-shortlisted / suggested contacts). Today interest shows where
employers already look: a count on the role page, a badge on the candidate view. The full
sections wait for the five founder interviews, per the 18 Oct sequencing decision.
