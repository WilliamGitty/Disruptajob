# Positioning

## The sentence

> **Disruptajob is where you're hired for what you've actually done, not what your job title says.**

If someone repeats only that back, we've succeeded. Everything below exists to defend it.

## The one-paragraph version

People increasingly do two or three jobs inside one role. A job title picks one of them and a CV
flattens the rest into a line. Disruptajob lets you describe real work you've done and tag what it
demonstrates — and that tagging is the only thing that moves your profile. Companies describe the
actual work a hire would own instead of writing a job spec, and we match the two. Candidates are
free, permanently. Companies pay.

## Who this is for

**Primary: the person whose title undersells them.** An "Operations Associate" who also builds the
automations and owns half of product. They're invisible to keyword search, they've sent a hundred
applications, and they've started to believe the problem is them.

They are not "talent". Nobody has ever described themselves as talent.

**Secondary: the company that keeps hiring the wrong person from the right CV.** Usually 10–200
people, where a bad hire is felt immediately and there's no recruitment function to hide behind.

## What we are not

Say these plainly when asked — the refusals are the positioning.

| Not | Because |
|---|---|
| A job board | Listing revenue would make us chase volume, and volume is the problem. |
| A CV builder | A better-formatted CV is still a CV. |
| An ATS | We stop at the conversation. What happens after is the company's system. |
| A recruiter | No fee per placement, no gatekeeping, no incentive to push a hire through. |
| An AI screening tool | We rank evidence, never people, and we show our reasoning every time. |

## Why now

AI writes a flawless cover letter in seconds, so polish stopped meaning anything. Every signal a
CV carried — fluency, structure, the right words — is now free to fake. **Proof of real work is the
only thing left that's hard to fake.** The thing that broke hiring is the thing that makes this
possible.

## The three proof points

Never make a claim without one of these underneath it.

1. **Demonstrated, never declared.** There is no column in our database for a self-declared score.
   It's a property of how the thing is built, not a promise.
2. **Hidden until you say otherwise.** A new profile is invisible. You can block your current
   employer by name, and they're never told. Enforced in the database, not in a policy.
3. **Every match is explainable.** "Evidence for 2 of 3 — Automation, Operations." You can open the
   project and judge it yourself.

## The competitive line

- **LinkedIn** digitised the CV. Same claims, better formatting, and endorsements anybody can give
  anybody.
- **Indeed** improved the job board. More listings, faster applications, worse odds.
- **Us:** neither. We start from the work.

## What we'd have to believe to be wrong

Stated honestly, because an investor will ask and the answer builds more credibility than a dodge.

- That enough people will write up their work — this is real effort, and it's our main risk.
- That companies will describe work instead of posting a job spec.
- That evidence beats credentials often enough to change who gets hired.
